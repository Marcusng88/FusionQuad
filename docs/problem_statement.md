THEME 1: Load Shifting & Peak Shaving  
Problem Statement
Commercial buildings and EV charging facilities in Malaysia are facing significantly higher electricity costs due to the sharp increase in Maximum Demand (MD) charges, rising from approximately RM30.30/kW to RM97.06/kW effective July 2025. Uncoordinated operation of high-power loads such as EV chargers, manufacturing machines, HVAC systems and etc. during peak periods leads to costly demand spikes. The key challenge is the lack of intelligent, predictive energy management capable of forecasting demand and autonomously performing load shifting or peak shaving to minimise operational costs under the current tariff structure.

EV Charging Stations (AC / DC Chargers)
Commercial Building Electrical Systems
Tariff Structures
Solar PV Systems (On-site Generation)

Background 
Global evidence shows that intelligent load management significantly improves energy consumption and cost savings. For example, Octopus Energy’s Agile tariff programme in the UK demonstrated that:
Customers shifted 28% of electricity consumption out of peak periods
EV drivers reduced peak consumption by 47%
Average savings were £188 per year compared to standard tariffs 
These results highlight that predictive, automated systems can convert pricing signals into operational savings.
In Malaysia, commercial facilities increasingly deploy EV chargers, solar PV, and EMS. However, energy usage remains largely reactive, and variable pricing structures alone do not guarantee cost reduction. Rising EV penetration and distributed solar generation add further variability to load profiles.
 
Machine Learning presents an opportunity to predict demand, optimise flexible loads, and evaluate whether load shifting or peak shaving can meaningfully reduce electricity costs under Malaysia’s variable pricing and operational conditions.
 
Expected Deliverables
Participants are required to develop an AI-based solution that:
Forecasts load demand using historical data
Identifies peak demand periods
Proposes automated load shifting strategy (including solar & battery sizing)
Simulates cost savings and peak reduction
Provides a dashboard or visual interface
 
Each team must propose ONE solution that demonstrates measurable impact.
